# P21 — Store Open-to-Close

Status: SOP-level business process specification · desk-research baseline

## 1. Purpose

Ensure each store opens ready to trade, remains controlled during the business day, and closes with operational, stock, cash, security and exception evidence completed.

## 2. Scope

Includes:

- pre-opening readiness;
- staffing/register readiness;
- cash/till readiness;
- store walk and safety/quality checks;
- price/promotion readiness;
- daily issue escalation;
- closing trade;
- till/shift handoff;
- store close checklist;
- unresolved exception handover;
- security/lock-up.

## 3. Actors

Store Manager, Duty Manager, Cashiers, Stock/Shelf Staff, Security/Loss Prevention, Cleaning/Maintenance, Regional Operations.

## 4. Opening evidence

- staffing/attendance confirmation;
- opening checklist;
- till/register readiness;
- float/handover record;
- critical equipment status;
- store safety/quality check;
- critical OOS/price/promo issue log.

## 5. Opening flow

1. Authorized manager enters/opens site.
2. Confirm security and critical utilities/equipment.
3. Confirm staffing and role coverage.
4. Complete food/quality/temperature checks where applicable.
5. Confirm registers/payment channels and opening float.
6. Walk sales floor/backroom for safety, cleanliness, shelf gaps and damage.
7. Confirm price/promotion changes due that day.
8. Confirm priority inbound/pickup/delivery tasks.
9. Escalate blockers.
10. Declare store ready to trade.

## 6. During-day control cycle

Monitor:

- queue/service level;
- shelf availability;
- price/promotion execution;
- receiving and backroom congestion;
- cash/safe-drop needs;
- quality/expiry exceptions;
- customer incidents;
- equipment/payment issues;
- suspicious refund/void/override;
- staffing gap.

Material issues enter an action/exception log with owner and status.

## 7. Closing flow

1. stop/limit customer entry according to policy;
2. complete active customer transactions;
3. secure customer pickup/staged orders;
4. complete or hand over pending receiving/stock tasks;
5. close cashier shifts and reconcile tills per P06;
6. secure cash/deposit evidence;
7. perform closing floor/backroom/safety check;
8. isolate waste/damage/quarantine requiring next action;
9. identify unresolved operational exceptions;
10. secure registers/devices/keys;
11. complete manager close sign-off;
12. secure/lock premises.

## 8. Handover / unresolved issues

Examples:

- tender mismatch;
- failed card settlement;
- stock discrepancy;
- fridge/temperature incident;
- pending supplier delivery;
- customer complaint;
- uncollected online order;
- safety/maintenance incident.

Each must have owner, evidence and next action; closing the store does not close the business exception.

## 9. Exceptions

- late/absent opener;
- register/payment outage;
- insufficient staffing;
- opening cash mismatch;
- critical equipment failure;
- safety/quality issue prevents opening;
- store cannot close because customers/incident remain;
- alarm/security failure;
- unresolved cash variance.

## 10. Controls

- opening/closing authority restricted;
- checklist completion attributable to responsible manager;
- critical safety/quality failures can block trading;
- cash close separated from self-approval for material variance;
- all open exceptions handed over;
- keys/access/device custody controlled;
- after-hours entry recorded.

## 11. KPIs

- on-time opening %;
- opening blocker rate;
- register availability;
- critical OOS at open;
- closing completion timeliness;
- unresolved exception aging;
- store incident rate;
- opening/closing checklist compliance.

## 12. Open retailer-policy questions

- opening/closing role authority;
- minimum staffing/role coverage;
- pre-open checklist by store format;
- critical equipment that can block opening;
- after-hours access policy;
- close checklist and unresolved exception SLA.
